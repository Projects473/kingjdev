Grenada Highlights Half-Day Rum, Chocolate and Waterfall Tour

Chocolate, rum and a waterfall in one half day.

The stops

  1. House of Chocolate — 30 minutes
    • Explore Grenada’s cocoa and chocolate heritage
    • Learn about local chocolate production
    • Chocolate sampling is included
  2. Clarke’s Court Rum Distillery — 35 minutes
    • Visit one of Grenada’s traditional rum distilleries
    • Learn about the rum-making process
    • Enjoy a rum tasting
  3. Annandale Waterfall & Forest Park — 30 minutes
    • Explore the tropical gardens surrounding the waterfall
    • Take photos
    • Enjoy a refreshing swim under the waterfall

Included

  • Private transportation
  • Air-conditioned vehicle
  • Bottled water
  • Alcoholic beverages and rum tasting
  • Entrance fees
  • Chocolate sampling

Tour flow: Hotel or cruise port pickup → House of Chocolate → Clarke’s Court Rum Distillery → Annandale Falls → return to pickup location. The tour is operated by King J’s Taxi Services and Tours and is offered as a private experience, so only your group participates.

Best of Grenada: Waterfall, Grand Etang & Chocolate Half Day Tour

Duration: approximately 3–4 hours.

The stops

  1. Annandale Waterfall & Forest Park — 30 minutes
    • Explore the beautiful waterfall and tropical surroundings
    • Opportunity for photos and a refreshing swim
    • Admission included
  2. House of Chocolate / Tri-Island Chocolate — 60 minutes
    • Learn about Grenada’s cocoa and chocolate-making heritage
    • See the chocolate-making process
    • Enjoy chocolate sampling
  3. Grand Etang National Park — 30 minutes
    • Visit the scenic Grand Etang crater lake
    • Enjoy the rainforest scenery and cool mountain atmosphere
    • Opportunity to spot local wildlife, including monkeys

Included

  • Private transportation
  • Air-conditioned vehicle
  • Admission fees
  • Bottled water
  • Cold towel
  • Chocolate sampling
  • Rum punch / alcoholic beverages

Tour route: Annandale Falls → Tri-Island Chocolate → Grand Etang Lake → return to pickup point.

Grenada Beach Escape, Waterfall, Rainforest Adventure

Duration: 4 hours.

The stops

  1. Grand Étang National Park — 30 minutes
    • Visit the scenic volcanic crater lake
    • Enjoy the cool rainforest atmosphere
    • Look out for Mona monkeys and other wildlife
    • Take photos of the surrounding rainforest
  2. Annandale Waterfall & Forest Park — 45 minutes
    • Explore the lush tropical surroundings
    • Enjoy the beautiful waterfall
    • Take photos
    • Opportunity for a refreshing swim
    • Admission included
  3. BBC Beach — 2 hours
    • Relax on the beautiful white-sand beach
    • Swim in the calm, crystal-clear Caribbean waters
    • Enjoy some free time to unwind and take in the scenery

Included

  • Private transportation
  • Air-conditioned vehicle
  • Bottled water
  • Admission and entrance fees
  • Rum punch
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Annandale Falls admission

Tour flow: Hotel, port or airport pickup → Grand Étang National Park → Annandale Waterfall → BBC Beach → return to pickup location.

# Editing your website

Everything on your website — the tours, the prices, the photos, your phone
number — can be changed by you, without touching any code. This guide walks
through it.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org**
2. Sign in with the email address your invitation was sent to.
3. Click the **King J's** website in the list.

You'll see a menu down the left with four sections:

- **Contact details & pop-up**
- **Tours**
- **Drivers**
- **Gallery**

Click a section, make your change, then click **Save** at the top right.
Your change is live on the website in a minute or two. Refresh the site to see it.

If it hasn't changed after a few minutes, open the site in a private browsing
window — your phone sometimes holds on to the old version for a while.

---

## Changing your phone number, WhatsApp or email

**Contact details & pop-up**

Change the field you need and save. It updates everywhere at once: the contact
page, the footer of every page, and every WhatsApp button on the site.

A note on the two phone fields:

- **Phone number (as it should appear)** is what visitors read, so write it the
  pretty way: `+1 (473) 419 8788`
- **WhatsApp number** is what the WhatsApp button dials, so it must be digits
  only with the country code and nothing else: `14734198788`

## The welcome pop-up

Same section, at the bottom, under **Welcome pop-up**.

- **Show the pop-up to visitors** — turn this off and the pop-up disappears
  from the site completely.
- **Big heading**, **Small heading**, **Text** — the words on the card.
- **Video** — click the field, then **Upload** to add a new clip.
- **Still image shown while the video loads** — a photo shown for the moment
  before the video starts. A frame from the video works best.
- **"Watch the reel" link** — paste the address of any Instagram post or reel.

Videos should be MP4. If your video comes off an iPhone and won't play on the
site, it's probably in Apple's format and needs converting first — send it over
and it can be converted for you.

## Changing a tour

**Tours**. Each tour is a row; click one to open it.

- **Tour name**, **Small heading** (the duration line), **Opening line**
- **Stops** — each stop has a name with its timing, like
  `House of Chocolate — 30 minutes`, and its bullet points underneath.
  Use **Add** to add a stop or a bullet, the handle at the left to drag them
  into a different order, and the bin to delete one.
- **What's included** — one line per item.
- **Route** — the line at the bottom of the tour.
- **Price** — type whatever you want shown, for example `US$120 per person` or
  `Price on request`.
- **Main photo** — the big photo beside the tour.
- **Photo strip under the tour** — the row of photos guests swipe through.

### Putting your prices up

The website currently says "Price on request" on every tour. To show real
prices, change two fields on each tour:

1. **Price** — shown on the tours page.
2. **Home page card — price** — the short version on the home page, for example
   `From US$120`.

### The home page cards

Three fields on each tour control its card on the home page: **Home page card —
small heading**, **short description** and **price**. The card's photo and title
come from the tour's own main photo and name, so those change together.

### Adding a new tour

At the bottom of the Tours list click **Add**, then fill it in. One field needs
care: **Page anchor**. It has to be lowercase with no spaces, for example
`sunset-cruise`, and it's what makes links to that tour work. Everything else is
plain writing.

## Drivers

**Drivers**. Each driver has a name, a role, a write-up, a closing line and
photos.

- **Show large at the top with a photo carousel** — this is what puts Joel in
  the wide card at the top with several photos. Everyone with this switched off
  appears in the smaller cards below with one photo each.
- To add a driver, click **Add** at the bottom of the list. To remove one, use
  the bin icon.

## Gallery

**Gallery**. The video at the top has its own heading and caption. Below it,
each album is a row: open it to rename it or change its photos.

Albums appear on the page in the order they're listed, and photos appear in the
order they're listed inside each album. Drag them to reorder.

To add a new album, click **Add** at the bottom, give it a name and add photos.

---

## Adding photos

Anywhere you see a photo field, click it and either pick a photo already on the
site or click **Upload** to add a new one from your phone or computer.

Underneath each photo there's a **Short description** box. Write what's in the
picture in a few words, like "Guests at Annandale Falls". Blind visitors' screen
readers read it out, and Google uses it, so it's worth filling in.

Photos straight from a phone camera are fine. Very large photos make the site
slower to load, so if you have a choice, send the "medium" or "large" size
rather than "actual size".

## Languages

Visitors can read the site in English, Spanish, French, Dutch or German using
the globe button at the top of every page. Their choice is remembered.

The translations are held separately from the forms you edit. So when you
change a tour description or add a new tour, it appears in English for everyone
until the translation is added. Send Greg a note when you've made a change
worth translating.

## If something goes wrong

Nothing you do here can break the design of the site, and every change is
recorded. If a change looks wrong, change it back and save — or ask Greg, who
can restore any earlier version of any page.

What isn't editable here: the layout, the colours, the logo, the menu, the map,
and adding new pages. Those come back to Greg.

Questions that come up.

How far ahead should I book?

Tours are worth a few days’ notice, especially in high season and around Spice Mas. Taxi runs are often same-day — one guest booked a tour less than 24 hours ahead and had it confirmed almost immediately.

How much notice for a pickup?

About thirty minutes is what regular guests are asked for. That is enough to have someone with you rather than you waiting.

What if my flight is delayed?

Send the flight number when you book. Arrivals get tracked, so a late landing does not mean an empty car park.

Can you take a big group?

Yes. The van seats twelve together. When Joel is already out, one of his own drivers covers the run and you will know who is coming.

Are entry fees included?

Site entry, guides at the falls and lunch are paid separately. Bring some small cash for those.

Do you do cruise ship days?

Pickup from the pier in St. George’s, timed to have you back well before the ship sails.

Is it suitable for kids?

It has been done plenty of times. Car seats and pace can be arranged — mention it when you book.

Can I pay by card?

Card or cash, in XCD or USD. Handy if you have just landed and have not found an ATM.

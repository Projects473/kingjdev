Our story

King J’s Taxi Service and Tours Grenada was built from small beginnings, driven by a simple belief: hard work, genuine hospitality and great customer service can turn a small dream into something extraordinary.

As a young entrepreneur, King J understood the value of putting customers first. From the beginning the goal has been to provide more than just transportation — it is about making every guest feel welcomed, comfortable, safe and appreciated.

Through dedication, consistency and a commitment to excellence, King J’s Taxi Service and Tours Grenada has grown into one of the most highly rated taxi and tour companies on the island, earning countless five-star reviews on Tripadvisor and Google.

Our mission

To provide reliable transportation and unforgettable experiences, while showcasing the beauty, culture and warmth of Grenada.

We stand for great service, punctuality, reliability, safety and genuine hospitality. Travelling from the airport, heading to your hotel, or exploring the island — we are committed to making your journey smooth, enjoyable and memorable.
